parcels.pmtiles
  Parcel boundaries: North Carolina counties via NC OneMap (NC1Map_Parcels).
  Attributes kept: parcel number (parno), county name, GIS acres. No owner,
  mailing, sale or value field is requested or published.
  NC OneMap: free to use without restriction; county data stewards are the
  authoritative source. Boundaries are tax-parcel representations, not surveys.

buildings.pmtiles
  Microsoft US Building Footprints (North Carolina). ODbL 1.0.
  Building heights (h) are stylised from footprint area, not measured.
